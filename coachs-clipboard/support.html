FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Your teams, rosters, and games are stored only on your device.

How do I move my teams to a new phone?

Data lives in the app's on-device store, so it comes along with an iPhone backup or device-to-device transfer. Deleting the app deletes your teams.

What does Pro include?

Unlimited teams, Message Team, sharing a rotation as an image or PDF, and no banner ads. Pro is $4.99 per month or $34.99 per year, with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Cancel anytime in Settings > your name > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. To restore on a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

Why is a player sitting more than others?

The rotation balances sits over the season. Check the Fairness tab, and use Regen on the game to rebuild a lineup. You can always edit positions by hand; the app warns you before breaking a rule.

Why doesn't the weather show?

Add a field address (or field name) to the team or game. The forecast comes from Open-Meteo and appears when the game is within the forecast window.

Which sports are supported?

Baseball, softball, basketball, soccer, lacrosse, floor hockey, and volleyball, for youth and adult leagues.
